What Is Rare Dungeons

A Friend — your character — enters a dungeon and must survive 3 floors. Each floor has enemies, fog, pit traps, and other hazards — all shaped by the perks the dungeon owner chose at creation. Before entering, the raider picks perks for the Friend. The dungeon owner picks perks for the dungeon itself. Both sides have 6 perks × 3 ranks. The same run, two opposite loadouts.

Raider pays entry and bets the Friend survives all 3 floors. Win → collect the bank. Lose → entry stays in the dungeon, dungeon level rises. Dungeon Owner paid to create. They bet raiders keep dying. Each failed raid grows the bank. Each win earns tickets toward the weekly pool jackpot.

PSYCHOLOGY — Why Players Come Back

Soft as a funnel. 33% wins feel real and frequent. Players experience winning before risking more. Then Hard calls.

Skill attribution — both sides. The raider has 6 perks × 3 ranks: Dash, Fearless, Endurance, Sharp Eye, Dodge, Rally. The dungeon owner has 6 perks × 3 ranks: Horde, Walls, Fog, Dreadful Beasts, Sharp Claws, Thick Hide. Hundreds of combinations. When you win, the build felt right. When you lose, the next build feels smarter.

Near-miss architecture. Dying on Floor 3 with 1 stamina left is the strongest retention moment in the game. Players don't quit — they reroll a perk and go again.

The × is always visible. Before every raid: TO WIN / YOUR × / TICKETS. The potential payout is never hidden. Live feed drops events every 10–15 seconds: "Friend earned 9.1× from raid." "Holder claimed +$147." The world feels active. You feel like you're missing out.
